Privacy Policy for Daily Budget

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Daily Budget ("the app") is a personal budgeting app for Android. This policy explains what data the app handles and, importantly, what it does not.

Data collection

Daily Budget does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no network access — it does not connect to the internet at all.

The optional Pro upgrade is bought through Google Play. The purchase happens in Google Play's own payment screen, and Google, not the app, handles your payment details (see "Third parties" below).

Data storage

All information you enter — your budget, currency, expenses, and settings — is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. A copy of your budget, recent expenses and display settings is also kept on-device so the morning notification and the home-screen widget can show today's number, along with whether Pro is unlocked. None of this data ever leaves your device, and we (the developer) have no access to it.

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data. Daily Budget does not provide cloud backup or sync.

Permissions

  • Notifications — used only to show the optional morning budget reminder you can enable in Settings.
  • Receive boot completed — used only to re-schedule that same reminder, and refresh the widget, after your device restarts or its clock/timezone changes.
  • Google Play billing — lets the app ask the Google Play app on your device to show its purchase screen for Pro, and to check whether you've bought it.
  • View network connections — added by Google's billing library. It only lets an app see whether the device is online; it does not allow sending or receiving anything, and Daily Budget does not use it.

None of these permissions is used to collect or transmit data.

Third parties

Pro purchases are processed by Google Play, under Google's Privacy Policy. Google shares with us the order information that Google Play provides to app sellers, which we use only to manage purchases and refunds. Daily Budget itself does not receive your payment details. Apart from Google Play's billing library, the app uses no third-party services, SDKs, or libraries, and the parts of that library that would send usage data over the internet are left out of the app.

Children's privacy

Daily Budget does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. The app itself collects no information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a revised "Last updated" date.
