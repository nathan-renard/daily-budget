Privacy Policy for Daily Budget

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Daily Budget ("the app") is a personal budgeting app for Android. This policy explains what data the app handles and, importantly, what it does not.

Data collection

Daily Budget does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no network access — it does not connect to the internet at all.

Data storage

All information you enter — your budget, currency, expenses, and settings — is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. A small copy of your reminder settings is also kept on-device so the app can schedule your morning notification. None of this data ever leaves your device, and we (the developer) have no access to it.

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data. Daily Budget does not provide cloud backup or sync.

Permissions

  • Notifications — used only to show the optional morning budget reminder you can enable in Settings.
  • Receive boot completed — used only to re-schedule that same reminder after your device restarts or its clock/timezone changes.

Neither permission is used to collect or transmit data.

Third parties

Daily Budget does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or libraries that collect data.

Children's privacy

Daily Budget does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a revised "Last updated" date.
